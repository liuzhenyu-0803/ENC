POSITIONS:
This cell has been positioned on the WGS-84 Datum with the use of 2018 orthorectified imagery.

CAUTION 1:
Fish Aggregating Devices (FADs), anchored or drifting, are commonly deployed within the waters of many Pacific Islands and may not be charted. Even though many of the installations are temporary in nature, FADs pose significant risk to navigation safety. Prudent mariners should exercise caution in navigating these waters.

CAUTION 2:
Navigators should observe extreme caution in this area. Hydrography and navigational aids have been adjusted to conform to the new shoreline and reef as taken from aerial photography and therefore cannot be totally relied upon.

SUBMARINE CABLES:
Mariners are advised not to anchor, trawl, or fish in the vicinity of submarine cables.
